• Disbursement Bottleneck & TAT (Turnaround Time) Report: Since you have 4 
applications marked as Pending disbursement out of 8 total, you need a report that tracks

policy over time.

initially but drop out before final review, helping you see if your process is too tedious 
for clients.

disbursed within 24–48 hours.

ACTIVE LOANS 
1. Fix Critical Data Bugs (UI & Backend Improvements) 
• Fix the Negative Recovery Rate: Your dashboard shows a Recovery Rate of -32.4%. 
A recovery rate should mathematically sit between 0% and 100%. A negative number 
implies you are losing principal that hasn't even been disbursed, or your calculation 
formula is subtracting outstanding interest incorrectly. 
• Fix the Epoch/Default Maturity Dates: Four of your pending loans show a maturity 
date of 01 Jan 1970. This is a classic Unix timestamp bug (0). The system should either 
hide the maturity date until the loan is active/disbursed, or calculate the expected maturity 
date dynamically based on the loan product terms once a start date is proposed. 
2. UI Layout & Action Elements 
• Dynamic Progress Bars: Your "Progress" column currently shows 0% paid in plain text 
for every single loan (even the active ones like Rosemary's and Mohamed's). Replacing 
this with a visual percentage progress bar (e.g., green for principal paid, gray for 
remaining) makes tracking loan lifecycles instant. 
• Principal vs. Total Outstanding Breakdown: For LN-APP-20260717-227180, the 
principal is TSh 200,000, but outstanding shows TSh 268,000. It would be helpful to split 
the "Outstanding" column into [Principal Remaining | Interest/Fees Accrued] so 
officers know exactly what makes up the balance. 
• Quick-Filter Tabs: Instead of just a single "All Status" dropdown, add quick-click 
metric tabs at the top for Active, Pending, and Overdue/Arrears so managers can filter 
the list with one click. 
 
3. Essential Reports to Launch From This Page 
• Expected Collections Calendar / Repayment Report: A forward-looking report 
showing exactly which loans are due for a payment today, this week, or this month. This 
is essential for your loan officers to plan their collection routes. 
• Portfolio at Risk (PAR) Aging Report: Even though you currently show 0 in 
arrears, as you scale past 7 loans, you will need a report that automatically buckets 
loans by how many days they are past due (1–30 days, 31–60 days, etc.). 
• Loan Product Performance Report: Compares your GROUP LOANS against INDIVIDUAL 
LOANS to see which product has a higher volume and better recovery rate.
